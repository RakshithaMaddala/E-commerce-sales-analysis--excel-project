## 🛒 E-commerce sales analysis-excel project

## 📌 Overview

- This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful visual insights to evaluate business performance, customer behavior, and operational efficiency.  
It enables stakeholders to monitor key metrics, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making through dynamic visualizations and interactive filtering.  

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total sales and profit performance across years and months  
- Identify top-performing regions, countries, and product categories  
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability  
- Understand customer purchasing behavior through channel and payment analysis  
- Examine the relationship between shipping time and sales performance  
- Track business growth trends (YoY analysis)  
- Build an interactive dashboard for easy business monitoring   

## 🛠️ Tools & Features Used  

- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts  
- Slicers for interactive filtering  
- Calculated Fields (Profit Margin, KPI metrics)  
- Conditional Formatting  
- Data Cleaning & Transformation  
- Dashboard Design & Layout Optimization  

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights  

- Year-wise and Month-wise Sales & Profit Analysis  
- Regional, Country, and City Performance Comparison  
- Product Category & Top Product Analysis  
- Channel and Payment Method Insights  
- Discount vs Profit Analysis  
- Shipping Time vs Sales Performance Analysis  
- Interactive dashboard with dynamic slicers  

## 📂 Dataset Information  

- The dataset contains transactional e-commerce data including:  
- id, Order ID, Order Date, Order month  
- Customer ID  
- Region, Country, City  
- Product Category & Product Name  
- Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit  
- Payment Method  
- Shipping Days  
- year, month  

## 💡 Key Insights  

- Business performance declined slightly in 2025 compared to 2024.  
- Certain regions consistently generate higher revenue and profit.  
- Sales show seasonal trends with peak performance during high-demand months.  
- Excessive discounts negatively impact profit margins.  
- Shipping time shows limited direct impact on sales performance.  
- High-demand markets benefit from consistent marketing engagement.  

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)  

- Total Sales & Total Profit  
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth  
- Profit Margin  
- Regional Performance  
- Channel Contribution  

## 🚀 Business Impact  

- This dashboard helps organizations:  
- Monitor business performance in real time

- Optimize marketing and operational strategies  
- Improve profitability through data-driven insights
